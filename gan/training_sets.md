# Training sets for the GAN

Datasets from Hugging Face that work well for GAN training:

- `huggan/metfaces`
- `huggan/pokemon`
- `huggan/flowers-102-categories`
- `huggan/inat_butterflies_top10k`
- `huggan/few-shot-art-painting`
- `huggan/anime-faces`

Or browse all datasets made for GAN training that contain between 1'000 and 10'000 images:
[huggingface.co/datasets (huggan, 1K–10K images)](https://huggingface.co/datasets?size_categories=or:%28size_categories:1K%3Cn%3C10K%29&sort=trending&search=huggan)

> ⚠️ **Important:** if you intend to exhibit work made with these datasets, check the license first! You find it on the dataset's page.
